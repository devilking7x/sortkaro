SortKaro — कचरा सही डिब्बे में

Which bin does it go in? Ask in English or Hindi — get the bin, the reason, and what to do next.

Built for Arbiter Hacks V1 — Theme Track: Rethinking Waste. Rules follow the bins most Indian cities use (Solid Waste Management Rules 2016). Your city may differ — the app says so where it matters.

2. Test yourself — the 10-item sorting quiz

Pick the right bin for 10 random household items. This is also how we measure whether SortKaro works: the quiz below is scored, and the classifier is tested on 20 item names it has never seen (see evaluation).

How we measured it

  • Classifier: 20 item names that are not in the 73-item list, each with a known correct bin. Result: … (recomputed live in your browser — open DevTools and run SortKaro.evaluateHeldOut()).
  • Quiz: 10 random items from the list; your score is shown immediately and your best score is saved on this device only.
  • Rules: every bin rule comes from the Solid Waste Management Rules 2016 and common ULB practice (green = wet/compostable, blue = dry/recyclable, red = hazardous, separate e-waste collection, yellow bag = sanitary). Cities differ — check your local collector for the grey areas.

Honest limits

  • SortKaro is a guide, not a government rulebook. Bin colours and rules vary by city and by housing society — when the app says “check locally”, it means it.
  • The classifier is rule-based, not AI: a 73-item hand-built list plus keyword rules. It does not recognise photos, brands, or items outside its list — it says “not sure” instead of guessing.
  • Nothing leaves your browser. No account, no server, no tracking. Your quiz best score is stored only in this browser's localStorage.
